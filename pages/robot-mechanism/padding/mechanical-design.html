IMU Sensor Mounting

Four MPU6050 6-axis IMU modules are distributed across three anatomical strike zones. Each sensor is positioned to detect impact acceleration when a user punches the corresponding zone of the padding.

IMU ID Zone I²C Address Mounting Orientation
IMU-0 Head (top) 0x68 (MUX Ch 0) Z-axis normal to strike surface
IMU-1 Head (side) 0x68 (MUX Ch 1) Z-axis normal to strike surface
IMU-2 Celiac Plexus 0x68 (MUX Ch 2) Z-axis normal to strike surface
IMU-3 Liver 0x68 (MUX Ch 3) Z-axis normal to strike surface

Each IMU is mounted with the Z-axis perpendicular to the local strike surface, ensuring maximum sensitivity along the primary impact direction. Wiring from each MPU6050 breakout board runs to the Teensy 4.1 via 30–40 cm 4-conductor ribbon cable (VCC, GND, SDA, SCL). All connections use JST-XH 2.54 mm connectors for tool-free disconnect during maintenance. The parasitic capacitance implications of these longer wire runs are addressed in the Electrical Integration page.
