5.2.3 Height Adjustment — Electrical & Control

Pending Implementation: The height-adjustment electrical and control subsystem is currently under development. This page provides a structural outline of the planned documentation. Content will be populated once the motor, driver, and control loop are fully integrated and validated.

Requirements Cascade

The height-adjustment electrical subsystem responds to the following system-level requirement defined in the Robot Mechanism section:

System Req Subsystem Implication
RM-3 ≥ 400 mm vertical stroke with ≤ 32 s full travel — motor must deliver ~800 rpm at the screw-jack input shaft
RM-1 Structural stability — self-locking screw jack must hold position without motor power under punching loads

Planned Documentation Outline

1. Motor Specification & Selection

24V DC gear motor (LGYMSZSS) driving the HK2T screw-jack input. Back-calculated 800 rpm speed requirement. Torque and current requirements at the jack under full payload.

2. Motor Driver — Cytron MDDS10

Sign-Magnitude mode operation. PWM (speed) and DIR (direction) pin assignments. Corrected wiring topology (Pin 3 → AN1, Pin 2 → DIG1). See Troubleshooting §5 for pin transposition defect history.

3. Self-Locking Safety

Inherent fail-safe behaviour from the screw-jack's travelling-nut design. Column holds position even without motor power, ensuring safety during training.

4. Control Strategy

Open-loop hold-to-move operation via GUI Height Tab. PWM ramp-down for regenerative back-EMF management. See Troubleshooting §6 for PSU OVP trip mitigation.

5. Power Integration

Connected to the 24V motor bus via the shared terminal block. Current draw profile during height adjustment operations.
