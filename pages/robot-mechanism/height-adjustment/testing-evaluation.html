Testing & Evaluation

Following the right side of the V-Model framework, this page presents the test and verification criteria for the height adjustment subsystem, evaluating the design against the original system requirement RM-3 and structural impact expectations.

1. Performance Criteria

ID Criterion Target
RM-3 Height adjustment stroke & Actuation time Provide ≥ 400 mm vertical stroke in ≤ 32 seconds.
RM-1 Structural stability & Self-locking Hold vertical position under punching loads without power. Telescopic column resists lateral punch loads without binding.

2. Test Procedures

Test 1: Full-Stroke Actuation (RM-3)

  • Setup: Robot fully assembled to ensure the screw jack is lifting the real-world mass.
  • Procedure: Command the motor from the minimum limit to the maximum limit. Measure physical travel distance and duration.
  • Success Criteria: Total measured stroke is ≥ 400 mm and total time is ≤ 32 seconds.

Test 2: Column Deflection & Self-Locking Hold

  • Setup: Robot fully assembled and raised to maximum stroke extension. Motor power off.
  • Procedure: Apply horizontal and vertical loads simulating user interaction to the top target pad.
  • Success Criteria: No permanent deformation of the mast, 0 mm vertical drift over 60 seconds (jack is self-locking), and the screw jack operates smoothly without binding after load removal.
