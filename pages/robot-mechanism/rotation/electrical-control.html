5.2.2 Rotation — Electrical & Control

Pending Implementation: The rotation electrical and control subsystem is currently under development. This page provides a structural outline of the planned documentation. Content will be populated once the motor, driver, and control loop are fully integrated and validated.

Requirements Cascade

The rotation electrical subsystem responds to the following system-level requirements defined in the Robot Mechanism section:

System Req Subsystem Implication
RM-4 Yaw angular velocity ≥ 150°/s — motor and drive ratio must deliver sufficient speed under load
RM-1 Hold commanded angle under punch impact — closed-loop position control with disturbance rejection

Planned Documentation Outline

1. Motor Selection & Specification

BLDC servo motor with integrated encoder. Selection rationale, torque/speed requirements, comparison with DC gear motor alternatives.

2. Communication Interface

CAN bus or RS485 integration with the main Teensy/Arduino controller. Protocol details, frame structure, and bus topology.

3. PID Cascade Control

Position → velocity → current cascade loops. Tuning methodology for smooth re-angling and disturbance rejection under punch impact. Reference to system-level PID cascade in Robot Mechanism §Architecture.

4. Power Integration

Connection to the system-level dual-rail power architecture (24V motor bus). Current draw profile during re-angling manoeuvres.
