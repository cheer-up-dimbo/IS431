**Summary of verification outcome**

At present, the base subsystem is **successful in concept, geometry,
structural direction, and mounting quality**, but **not yet fully closed
in physical verification**. The most important completed successes are:

-   the move to a proper welded steel base,

-   the trapezoidal footprint logic,

-   the rigid mounted plate,

-   and the low centre-of-mass strategy.

The most important remaining work is:

-   physical tip-and-roll testing,

-   full assembled tipping validation with the realised mass
    distribution,

-   and longer-term durability validation under real use and
    environmental exposure.
