---
name: Report Style Rules
description: Hard formatting and voice rules for all pages in the IS431/CDE4301 boxing robot report
type: feedback
---

No em dashes anywhere in prose or tables. Use colons, commas, or short sentences instead.

**Why:** Consistent house style across the report.

**How to apply:** Before finishing any edit, scan for -- and replace with a period or colon.

---

No name-dropping teammates. Refer to subsystems instead (e.g. "the CV subsystem", not "Yogee's subsystem").

**Why:** Engineering report convention; keeps the document professional.

**How to apply:** Any person-name reference must be replaced with their subsystem or role.

---

No emojis anywhere, including tables.

**Why:** Engineering project report, not a presentation slide.

---

Engineering project voice, not overly academic. Must be explainable in an oral exam.

**Why:** The document is assessed in an oral exam setting.

**How to apply:** Write as if explaining to an examiner. No jargon chains. No hedging phrases like "it is worth noting that" or "it can be observed that".

---

No AI filler phrases (e.g. "it is important to note", "this is crucial", "in order to", "leveraging", "delve into").

---

Inline citations use (Author, Year) format. Multiple authors: (Author and Author, Year) or (Author et al., Year).

---

Color-coded concept evaluation tables: green (#e8f5e9) = selected, red (#ffebee) = rejected. First column of the selected row is bold. No separate "Decision" column.

---

Navigation buttons at bottom of sub-pages follow Back/Next pattern.

---

Sub-headings (h4) must break up any section longer than two paragraphs.
