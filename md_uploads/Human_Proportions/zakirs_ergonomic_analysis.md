## 1. Landmark Definitions Used in the Final Report

The final report uses the following terminology consistently:

| Term | Definition | Use in design |
|---|---|---|
| Pronasale | Anatomical nose tip landmark | Upper reference for the head-to-body gap chain |
| Subxiphoid or epigastric region | Soft tissue immediately below xiphoid process | Central torso strike reference |
| Solar plexus | Internal celiac plexus, not a surface landmark | Colloquial boxing term only |
| Xiphoid process | Palpable lower sternum tip | Skeletal anchor landmark |

The phrase solar plexus may be retained only as boxing shorthand in brackets after epigastric or subxiphoid region.

## 3. ANSUR II as Primary Reference

The final section uses ANSUR II as the primary population basis and Drillis and Contini constants only where ANSUR II does not directly tabulate a landmark.

| Step | Method |
|---|---|
| Pronasale height | Estimated as 0.870H (Drillis and Contini, 1966) |
| Xiphoid proxy | Estimated from ANSUR II chestheight and tenthribheight midpoint |
| Gap used for design | Nose tip to subxiphoid or epigastric chain |

Final design reference from the section:
- Nominal head-to-body chain: about 31 cm
- Population variation band across 150 to 190 cm: about 5 cm

## 4. Current Body-Pad Layout Decision

After review of the anatomical visuals and the design intent, the body-pad narrative has been reframed as:

1. Epigastric pad is the **central reference pad**.
2. Liver pad is placed in a **lower lateral band** relative to the epigastric centreline.
3. The mirrored left pad is treated as **stomach-side** and kept level with the liver pad for symmetry.

This is an engineering layout choice for repeatable training geometry, while still grounded to central epigastric reference and lateral torso targets.
